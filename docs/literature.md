# Literature context

The manuscript concerns nonnegative factorizations of $0$-$1$
polynomials, with emphasis on trinomial factors of the form

$$
1+a x^2+x^v.
$$

The closest sources are:

- Luca Ghidelli, *Progress on the unfair 0–1 polynomials conjecture using
  linear recurrences and numerical analysis*,
  [arXiv:2209.09843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09843). This treats the fixed
  trinomial $1+a x^2+x^5$.
- Kevin G. Hare, *Computational progress on the unfair 0–1 polynomial
  conjecture*, *Experimental Mathematics*,
  [doi:10.1080/10586458.2025.2527786](https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2025.2527786).
  This studies bounded-degree factor patterns using a different computational
  framework.
- Musbahu Idris and Jean-Marc Sac-Épée, *Algorithmic aspects of Newman
  polynomials and their divisors*,
  [arXiv:2601.11486](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11486).
- Musbahu Idris and Jean-Marc Sac-Épée, *On the existence of Newman and
  Littlewood multiples for certain integer polynomials*,
  [arXiv:2607.15520](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15520).
- P. Drungilas, J. Jankauskas, and J. Šiurys, *On Newman and Littlewood
  multiples of Borwein polynomials*,
  [arXiv:1609.07295](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07295).

The Idris–Sac-Épée and Drungilas–Jankauskas–Šiurys papers address integer
divisors or restricted-coefficient multiples. They are adjacent to the present
work after conjugate norms turn a real factor into an integer divisor, but
they do not provide the distinguished-real-embedding parity-block argument
used in the manuscript.

The novelty claim is deliberately narrow: the searches represented above did
not reveal the all-odd-$v$ cubic and quartic exclusions or the exact
parity-block certificates developed here. This is literature-search evidence,
not an assertion of absolute priority.
